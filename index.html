Projects About Us Announcements

Building with Purpose,
Creating Enduring Value.

VS Infratech develops well planned, compliant, and future ready land developments that support sustainable economic and community growth.

Our integrated projects, VS Industrial Park and VS Residency, span over 35 acres of connected land in Hansi. Designed as a unified ecosystem, they enable businesses to operate efficiently while allowing families to build homes close to opportunity.

VS Industrial Park was created to address the shortage of approved industrial and commercial spaces in the city. With gated access, wide internal roads, organized plots, and essential infrastructure, it offers entrepreneurs a structured environment to start and grow their businesses with confidence.

Alongside it, VS Residency provides a thoughtfully planned residential township, creating a balanced live work ecosystem. Both developments are strategically located just 500 meters from NH9, ensuring strong connectivity.

The name VS is derived from Sh. VeerBhan Uthra and Sh. Shiv Dayal Bhatia, reflecting the values of integrity, responsibility, and long term thinking that guide every development at VS Infratech.
